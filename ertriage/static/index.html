How timing is measured

The onset proxy is the first observed 0-to-1 label transition plus 6 hours. The main warning window is 12 to 6 hours before that proxy. Records need a full window and the proxy inside the stay.

Positive-at-start, incomplete and nonpersistent records are counted separately. This is a retrospective analysis convention.
