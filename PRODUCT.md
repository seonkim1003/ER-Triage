# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The project owner and research collaborators inspecting historical ICU model behavior on a local computer. The immediate job is to understand what the existing prototype does, inspect a patient's timeline, and compare warning timing with review workload.

## Product Purpose

Provide a visual replay of the existing offline research pipeline: recorded vital signs, frozen model scores, threshold alerts, and illustrative review events. This is not a live patient monitor or an ER validation study. Use local computing without paid services.

## Design Principles

Show time and missing observations explicitly. Distinguish an alert from a review. Hide future scores and outcomes during ordinary playback. Make retrospective outcome inspection an explicit choice. Keep evaluation definitions and denominators available. Use recorded patient identifiers without adding identity information.

## Implementation Defaults

Plain, precise research language; standard keyboard-accessible controls; no emergency-monitor styling, alarm sounds, risk percentages, or treatment suggestions. These are implementation defaults for this prototype, not a separate brand brief.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Visible focus, text labels alongside chart marks, keyboard playback and scrubbing, reduced-motion support, responsive layout, and an hourly table as an alternative to charts.
