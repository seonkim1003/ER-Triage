# Research replay design

A researcher sits at a laptop in a bright study room, reading long timelines deliberately. Use a light paper-like neutral surface, dark graphite text, and restrained indigo for playback and model scores. Amber means a threshold alert; graphite dots mean reviews. Color never stands alone.

Use system sans-serif typography, tabular numerals, thin dividers, and flat sections. The main timeline receives the most space. A narrow left rail holds record selection and the current review explanation. The page header establishes research scope once. Playback controls sit immediately above the timeline. On small screens the rail stacks above the charts.

Use OKLCH color tokens, native inputs and buttons, visible focus rings, and no decorative animation. Patient loading and request failures appear inline. Retrospective mode adds explicit explanatory text and timing markers without changing any score or review event.
